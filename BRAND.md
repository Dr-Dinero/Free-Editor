# Brand pack

Copy this file into each video project (`video-projects/<slug>/BRAND.md`) and fill it in. The
editor follows it over every default. Leave a line blank to keep the calm default shown.

## Look

- Caption font: Figtree ExtraBold
- Title font: Libre Caslon Text
- Text color: #F4EFE6 (warm white)
- Accent color (one key word per caption line, title rule): #C9A45C (muted gold)
- Caption case: as spoken (not all caps)
- Caption size: about 80 px at 1080 wide
- Motion: calm (fade and rise; nothing bounces, slams, or shakes)

## Edit

- Energy: calm teaching (or: high energy, event promo, testimony)
- Cut zooms / punch-ins allowed: no
- Sound effects allowed: no
- Music level: 18 to 22 dB under the voice
- Default length: 30 to 60 s, main cut always under 90 s
- Ending: 1 s hold after the last word, then a 1 s fade
